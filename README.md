# ADAM Lab AI

**Autonomous Decision-Making and AI Research Lab**

Welcome to ADAM Lab AI — a research organization focused on advancing the frontier of autonomous, adaptive, and decision-making AI systems.

---

## 🧠 What We Do

ADAM Lab AI conducts foundational and applied research at the intersection of **machine learning**, **autonomous systems**, and **decision theory**. We build AI that can reason, plan, and act intelligently in complex, real-world environments.

Our work spans:

- **Reinforcement Learning & Planning** — agents that learn optimal behavior through interaction and experience
- **Large Language Models (LLMs)** — applying and extending language models for reasoning, tool use, and agentic workflows
- **Multi-Agent Systems** — designing agents that collaborate, compete, and communicate effectively
- **Robustness & Safety** — ensuring AI systems behave reliably and predictably under distribution shift and adversarial conditions
- **Human-AI Interaction** — building systems that augment human decision-making rather than replace it

---

## 🎯 Our Goals

1. **Advance Autonomous AI Research** — Publish rigorous, reproducible research that pushes the boundaries of what autonomous systems can do.
2. **Build Open, Reusable Tools** — Develop open-source frameworks and benchmarks that the broader research community can build upon.
3. **Bridge Theory and Practice** — Translate theoretical insights into practical systems with real-world impact.
4. **Foster a Collaborative Community** — Cultivate an inclusive environment where researchers, engineers, and students can contribute and grow together.
5. **Prioritize Responsible AI** — Embed safety, fairness, and transparency into every stage of research and development.
